Why I use the things I do

Why Spongebob?

I started using spongebob partway through my undergrad, for an assignment where we had to save and transfer data. Images provide a reliable way to determine if you're handling data correctly across multiple saves, moves, and fetches, since if you mess up, you'll be able to see where and how things went wrong. I began using a spongebob image because I thought it was funny, and becasue the image in question was just large enough to provide a sizeable source of data without being so large it would slow loading times. Plus Spongebob was one of my favorite shows as a kid, so there's that, too. Since then, I've used spongebob as my default for any coding project, whether I need an example image, theme, or text block. Including for this one!

Why These Colors?

No reason in particular. I chose this color scheme because it's fall, and I thought that this was a warm, fall-themed palette. For the other page, I used blues and greens to imitate being underwater- Spongebob does live under the sea, after all!
