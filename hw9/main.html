Say Hello to Spongebob Squarepants!






Patrick is Spongebob's best friend! He lives under a rock (literally) and isn't too bright, but he enjoys spending time with his best bud, jellyfishing, and having fun!

Mr. Krabs is Spongebob's boss at the Krusty Krab. His hobbies include making money, looking at money, pinching pennies, and underpaying his employees. Arg Arg Arg!

Squidward is Spongebob's neighbor. He enjoys playing the clairnet (poorly), interpretive art, and being better than everyone else. His least favorite thing in the world is Spongebob.




As the fry cook at the Krusty Krab, Spongebob is in charge of making the Krabby Patties. They consist of buns, patties, condiments, and kelp! The recipe for the patties themselves is a closely guarded secret, known only by Mr. Krabs himself.
