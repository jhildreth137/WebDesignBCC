Example of repetition

metazooa.com

This one uses an ordred list

  1. This website is a daily game where you guess an animal based on its order, class, species, etc.
  2. The repetition here comes from the design of the tree that is formed as you guess
  3. As each guess brings you closer to the answer, the tree forms a repetetive, scale-like shape (the more guesses you make, the more apparent this is)
  4. the simplicity of the site highlights the small bit of interesting repetition that you can only find by playing

Example of Alignment:

This one uses a description list (couldn't figure out how to center this one properly)

Overall take
The LEGO.com site does a good job of keeping elements aligned, with rows and boxes holding everything in place, despite varying sizes among objects.
Keeping it Blocky
Sonewhat ironic given their status as a toy block company, the site's use of boxes to organize elements keeps the field clean, and does not come across as unorganized.
Poor Proximity
However, this website is a poor example of proximity- there are many different elements of different types one after another, which can come across as cluttered, especially when scrolling.
