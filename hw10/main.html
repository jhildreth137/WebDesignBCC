Learn about the residents of Bikini Bottom!



Meet Spongebob Squarepants

Spongebob is the fry cook at the Krusty Krab. He lives in a pineapple under the sea, and enjoys spending time with his friends, jellyfishing, and all kinds of other silly things!

Meet Patrick Star

Patrick is Spongebob's best friend! He lives under a rock (literally) and isn't too bright, but he enjoys spending time with his best bud, jellyfishing, and having fun!

Meet Mr. Krabs.

Mr. Krabs is Spongebob's boss at the Krusty Krab. His hobbies include making money, looking at money, pinching pennies, and underpaying his employees. Arg Arg Arg!

Meet Plankton!

Plankton is the archnemesis of Mr Krabs, and the smartest single-celled organism in Bikini Bottom. He is always on the hunt for the secret Krabby Patty formula.
